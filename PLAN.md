# Game plan

An original VR action RPG with MMO features, inspired by "trapped in a VR MMO" anime.
It has its own world, name and characters (working title: **Spire VR**, a tower of floors floating above the clouds).

**Platform:** Meta Quest (standalone) · **Engine:** Godot 4 (OpenXR) · **Approach:** solo first, MMO later.

## Phase 1: "Hold a sword" ✅ prototype built
- [x] Floating island arena
- [x] Sword in the right hand; damage depends on swing speed; haptics
- [x] Enemies (one model, different colours) that chase, wind up and lunge
- [x] Damage numbers, HP bars, death shatter effect, kill counter
- [x] Thumbstick movement and snap turn; desktop test mode
- [ ] **Playtest on the Quest:** does swinging feel good? Tune the numbers.
- [ ] Grab and drop the sword (weapon rack) instead of it being stuck to the hand

## Phase 2: One complete floor
- Enemies with real animations (a rigged model), and more than one enemy type
- Player HP, XP, levelling up and stat points
- **Sword skills:** hold a stance, the blade glows, and a guided combo move fires
- Loot drops, inventory and equipment, opened from a hand-gesture floating menu
- Town hub → field → dungeon → **floor boss**
- Saving progress

## Phase 3: Co-op
- 2–4 player parties and a shared boss fight

## Phase 4: MMO layer
- Persistent online accounts, guilds, trading, a player market, more floors and events

## Open decisions
- Final game name and world lore
- Art style (low-poly for now)
- Death rules: casual or hardcore
